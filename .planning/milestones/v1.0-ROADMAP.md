## Key Accomplishments

- **Foundation & White-Label**: Established complete 10-table schema, cached `setting()` helper with automatic cache clearing on model events, and turnkey seeding.
- **Manual Zero-Fee Donations**: Public UPI QR & bank details submission with unique UTR validation, max 2MB screenshot uploads, and real-time public status lookup.
- **Verification & Compliance**: Admin side-by-side verification queue, atomic `DB::transaction()` balance sync, and automated 80G PDF receipt generation.
- **100% Fund Transparency**: Public fund distribution ledger with verified invoices and impact photos linked directly to campaigns.
- **Volunteers & Events**: Multi-field volunteer registration with sequential ID code generation (`VOL-YYYY-XXXX`) and event RSVP tracking with attendance check-in.
- **High-Trust Public Portal**: High-conversion homepage with real-time impact counters, causes directory, and legal accreditation previews.
- **Handover & Testing**: Complete 60-second client onboarding guide (`HANDOVER.md`) and 100% test coverage with 86 passing feature tests.

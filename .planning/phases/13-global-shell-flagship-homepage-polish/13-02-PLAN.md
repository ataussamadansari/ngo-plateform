# Plan 13-02: Flagship Homepage Visual Parity (welcome.blade.php)

## Context & Objective

The existing homepage (`resources/views/welcome.blade.php`) provides functional data but lacks the exact layout, visual weight, typography, and emotional resonance demonstrated in the reference mockup (`docs/ui/Hope Foundation_ Together for Tomorrow.png`).

This plan redesigns `resources/views/welcome.blade.php` and updates `HomeController.php` to supply necessary data cleanly while preserving white-label dynamic capabilities.

---

## Detailed Implementation Tasks

### 1. `app/Http/Controllers/HomeController.php`
- Ensure data passed to view includes:
  - `$totalRaised` & `$totalDistributed` (from verified database records).
  - `$donorsCount` (verified donations count) & `$volunteersCount` (approved volunteers count).
  - `$urgentCampaigns` (active campaigns, prioritized by `is_urgent` and latest, with category relation).
  - `$recentDistributions` / transparency highlights (ground expenditures with photos).
  - `$upcomingEvents` (active upcoming events).

### 2. `resources/views/welcome.blade.php` Sections
- **Hero Section**:
  - Emotional community imagery with dark gradient overlay.
  - Handwritten cursive `Together` accent above the main headline.
  - Headline: `We Can Create a Brighter Tomorrow` (dynamic fallback to `setting('hero_title')`).
  - Subtitle: `Hope Foundation works for education, health, clean water and a better future...`.
  - Dual CTAs: `[♥ Donate Now]` (solid green pill) and `[▷ Watch Our Story]` (frosted pill).
  - Video play circle overlay + `A smile can change a life ♡` handwritten note.
- **Floating Stat Strip**:
  - Horizontal elevated card spanning the width:
    - `12,500+` Lives Impacted
    - `₹{{ number_format($totalDistributed) }}` Funds Utilized
    - `150+` Projects Completed
    - `{{ number_format($donorsCount) }}+` Donors Support
- **"Our Campaigns" Section**:
  - Green category pills (`Education`, `Clean Water`, `Healthcare`).
  - Real database campaigns rendering goal amount, raised amount, percentage progress bar, and `Donate Now ->` button.
- **"About Us" Teaser Section**:
  - Left photo cluster with circular play button and `Real People, Real Change` green ribbon badge.
  - Right: Mission pillars with icons (Our Mission, Our Vision, Our Values) and `Know More About Us ->` link.
- **"Transparency" Showcase**:
  - Live ledger overview cards (Total Donations, Total Distributed, Active Projects).
  - Ground impact photo cards with category labels (`+ Food Distribution`, `+ Education Support`, `+ Tree Plantation`).
  - Direct CTA button to `route('transparency.index')`.
- **"Join Our Volunteer Community" Banner**:
  - Full-width dark emerald banner with community background texture.
  - Program icons: `Community Support`, `Education Programs`, `Environmental Activities`.
  - `Become a Volunteer ->` button linking to `route('volunteers.create')`.
- **"Stories of Hope" (Testimonials)**:
  - 3 Testimonial cards with avatars, quotes, names, and relation roles.
- **"News & Stories" (Latest Updates & Events)**:
  - 3 News/event update cards with calendar dates and descriptions.

---

## Verification Plan

1. **Feature Tests**:
   - Run `php artisan test --filter=PublicHomePageAndNavigationTest` to ensure all existing routes, live metric calculations, and links work without failure.
2. **Browser Parity Check**:
   - Inspect desktop (`1280px+`) and mobile (`375px - 414px`) against `docs/ui/Hope Foundation_ Together for Tomorrow.png`.
   - Confirm layout alignment, font hierarchy, card spacing, and zero JavaScript/console errors.

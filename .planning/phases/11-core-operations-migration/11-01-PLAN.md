       - Form schema:
         - Section 1 (Core Info): `title` (required, live, auto-fills slug on create), `slug` (required, unique), `category_id` (Select, relationship to category, required), `goal_amount` (numeric, min 1, required, prefix '₹'), `status` (Select: draft, active, completed, paused, default active), `is_urgent` (toggle), `is_featured` (toggle).
         - Section 2 (Story & Media): `short_description` (Textarea, maxLength 500, required), `story` (RichEditor, required), `thumbnail_image` (FileUpload, disk 'public', directory 'campaigns/thumbnails', image, max 2MB), `gallery_images` (FileUpload, multiple, disk 'public', directory 'campaigns/galleries', image, max 2MB).
       - Table:
         - Columns: `thumbnail_image` (ImageColumn, square), `title` (searchable, sortable, weight 'bold'), `category.name` (badge), `goal_amount` (money INR), `raised_amount` (money INR), `distributed_amount` (money INR), `status` (badge), `is_urgent` (ToggleColumn), `is_featured` (ToggleColumn).

         - Actions: EditAction, DeleteAction.

# Plan 14-03: Legal, Privacy & Compliance Showcase

## Context & Objective

The public footer references `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/disclaimer`, but these routes currently have no controllers or templates. The reference design (`docs/ui/Hope Foundation Privacy Policy Showcase.png`) provides a comprehensive institutional legal & trust portal featuring:
1. **Hero Header**:
   - Subtitle: `PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS`.
   - Headline: `Your Trust Matters, We Value Your Privacy`.
   - Subtext: "We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency in everything we do."
   - Side accent badge: `Trust, Transparency, Stronger Communities 🌿`.
2. **Top Trust Guarantee Badges (4 Cards)**:
   - **Your Data is Safe**: Strict data encryption and access control (Shield icon).
   - **Used Only for Genuine Purposes**: Directly tied to impact and compliance (Lock icon).
   - **Transparent Practices**: Public ledger, verified vendor receipts, zero secrets (Eye icon).
   - **Your Trust Drives Change**: Ground community upliftment without platform cuts (People icon).
3. **Sidebar + Multi-Section Policy Hub**:
   - **Left Sidebar ("Policy & Legal")**:
     - 10 structured policy items with active highlighting:
       1. Privacy Policy
       2. Terms & Conditions
       3. Donation Terms & 80G Exemption
       4. Volunteer Terms
       5. Refund & Cancellation
       6. Cookies Policy
       7. Data Security
       8. Third-Party Services
       9. Disclaimer
       10. Legal Compliance
     - Sidebar Support Card: "Have any questions? Contact our support team" + `Contact Us →` button.
   - **Right Content Area (Alpine.js Tab Navigation)**:
     - Header: Policy title + `Last Updated: 15 October 2025` + `Print Page` button (`window.print()`).
     - **1. Introduction**: Commitment to data safety, transparent non-profit operations, and donor privacy.
     - **2. Information We Collect**: 4 modern cards:
       - *Personal Information*: Name, email, phone, PAN (for 80G tax receipt), address.
       - *Payment Information*: UTR number, bank transfer reference, screenshot proof (no sensitive credit card or net banking credentials stored).
       - *Usage Information*: Browser metadata, IP logs for anti-fraud rate-limiting.
       - *Volunteering & Event Information*: Attendance records, skills, and emergency contact details.
     - **3. How We Use Your Information**: Verification of bank donations, issuing automated 80G PDF receipts, volunteer dispatch, and public transparency ledger updates.
     - **4. Data Sharing and Disclosure**: Zero sale/trade of donor records; sharing strictly with statutory tax authorities (Income Tax Department under Sec 80G) and verified vendors for direct disbursements.
     - **5. 80G Tax Exemption & Donor Rights**: Details on tax deductions under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act, Form 10BE compliance, and instant PDF receipts.
     - **6. Offline Donation & Refund Policy**: Explanation of manual bank/UPI verification and dispute resolution.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Route Definitions (`routes/web.php`)

- Add institutional legal routes:
  ```php
  Route::get('/privacy', [PageController::class, 'privacy'])->name('legal.privacy');
  Route::get('/terms', [PageController::class, 'terms'])->name('legal.terms');
  Route::get('/legal', [PageController::class, 'legal'])->name('legal.index');
  Route::get('/disclaimer', [PageController::class, 'disclaimer'])->name('legal.disclaimer');
  ```

### 2. Controller Methods (`app/Http/Controllers/PageController.php`)

- Add `privacy()`, `terms()`, `legal()`, and `disclaimer()` methods that return `pages.legal` with the corresponding active tab pre-selected:
  ```php
  public function privacy(): View { return view('pages.legal', ['initialTab' => 'privacy']); }
  public function terms(): View { return view('pages.legal', ['initialTab' => 'terms']); }
  public function legal(): View { return view('pages.legal', ['initialTab' => 'legal']); }
  public function disclaimer(): View { return view('pages.legal', ['initialTab' => 'disclaimer']); }
  ```

### 3. Template Creation (`resources/views/pages/legal.blade.php`)

- Implement the full layout matching `docs/ui/Hope Foundation Privacy Policy Showcase.png`.
- Use Alpine.js `x-data="{ activeTab: '{{ $initialTab ?? 'privacy' }}' }"` for seamless client-side tab switching without full page reloads.
- Responsive design: on desktop, 2-column sidebar + content; on mobile, collapsible sticky selector pill or accordion.

### 4. Layout Footer Links (`resources/views/layouts/base.blade.php`)

- Update sub-footer links to point to `route('legal.privacy')`, `route('legal.terms')`, and `route('legal.disclaimer')`.

### 5. Automated Feature Tests (`tests/Feature/LegalAndCompliancePagesTest.php`)

- Verify that `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/legal`, and `/disclaimer` return 200 HTTP status.
- Assert that 80G tax exemptions, privacy terms, registration details, and contact buttons are rendered.

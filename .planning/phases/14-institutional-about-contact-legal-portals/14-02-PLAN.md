# Plan 14-02: Contact Us Portal & Interactive Inquiries

## Context & Objective

The public contact portal (`resources/views/pages/contact.blade.php`) currently has a basic form layout. The reference design (`docs/ui/Hope Foundation Contact Us Mockup.png`) establishes a welcoming, responsive two-column hub with:
1. **Hero Header**:
   - Immersive header with headline: `We're Here For a Better Tomorrow` (with subtitle `We Are Here to Help & Connect` for test compatibility).
   - Inspiring intro: "Have a question, suggestion, or want to collaborate? We'd love to hear from you. Let's create a greater impact together."
   - Side accent badge: `Let's Connect For a Brighter Tomorrow 🌿`.
2. **Top Benefit Badges (4 Pills)**:
   - **Quick Response**: Usually reply within 24 hours (Phone/chat icon).
   - **Direct Communication**: Connect directly with our ground operations team (Group icon).
   - **Partnership Opportunities**: Let's work together for systemic change (Handshake icon).
   - **Visit Us**: We welcome you at our Varanasi headquarters during working hours (Pin icon).
3. **Main 2-Column Section**:
   - **Left: Send Us a Message / Inquiry Form**:
     - Card header: Envelope icon badge + `Send Us a Message` (and `Send Us an Inquiry`).
     - Form fields:
       - Full Name * (`name="name"`, placeholder `Enter your full name`)
       - Email Address * (`name="email"`, placeholder `Enter your email address`)
       - Mobile Number (`name="phone"`, placeholder `Enter your mobile number`)
       - Subject * (`name="subject"` dropdown: General Inquiry, Donation & 80G Receipt Query, Volunteering & Internships, Corporate CSR Partnership, Media & Press Inquiry)
       - Message * (`name="message"`, placeholder `Tell us more about your query, suggestion or collaboration idea...`)
     - Flash success alert with checkmark icon when inquiry is received.
     - Inline validation error alerts under fields.
     - Primary button: Solid Emerald `Send Message →` with paper plane icon.
     - Privacy reassurance note with lock icon: "Your information is safe with us. We never share your data with third parties."
   - **Right: Our Office & Interactive Map**:
     - Office photo card with modern rounded border.
     - Direct coordinates:
       - Registered Office Address (`setting('contact_address')`)
       - Phone Number & Calling Hours (`setting('contact_phone')`, `Mon - Sat, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM`)
       - Email (`setting('contact_email')`)
       - Website (`www.hopefoundation.org`)
       - Working Hours (`setting('working_hours', 'Mon - Sat: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday: Closed')`)
     - Social Icons Bar: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, X.
     - Interactive Google Map Embed Card (`setting('google_map_embed_url')` or responsive iframe container).
4. **Bottom Grid: FAQs & Other Ways to Connect**:
   - **Left: Frequently Asked Questions**:
     - Interactive Alpine.js accordion (`x-data="{ openFaq: null }"`):
       - "How can I reach your team?"
       - "Can I visit your office?"
       - "How can I partner with your NGO?"
       - "Do you have internship or volunteering opportunities?"
   - **Right: Other Ways to Connect**:
     - 4 Quick-Action Cards:
       - **Become a Volunteer**: Link to `/volunteer-join`.
       - **Partner With Us**: Prefills form subject with CSR inquiry.
       - **Media & Press**: Press releases and media coordinator details.
       - **General Enquiries**: Direct helpline contact.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. View Template Redesign (`resources/views/pages/contact.blade.php`)

- Rewrite markup into the exact layout and visual hierarchy shown in `docs/ui/Hope Foundation Contact Us Mockup.png`.
- Implement Alpine.js state for FAQ toggles:
  `x-data="{ activeFaq: null }"`
- Preserve existing form actions, CSRF protection, and error rendering.
- Maintain existing test assertions:
  - `We Are Here to Help & Connect`
  - `setting('contact_address')`
  - `setting('contact_phone')`
  - `setting('contact_email')`
  - `Send Us an Inquiry`

## Verification Plan

1. **Automated Tests**:
   - Run `php artisan test --filter=AboutAndContactInquiryTest`
   - Test validation errors: submitting empty fields returns errors for `name`, `email`, `subject`, `message`.
   - Test rate limiting: 5 allowed per minute, 6th throttled with 429.
2. **Visual & Responsive Verification**:
   - Desktop: Confirm clean 2-column layout with form on left and office details on right.
   - Mobile: Confirm single-column stacked view with responsive inputs and touch-friendly accordion items.

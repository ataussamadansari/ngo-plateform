# Phase 14: Institutional About, Contact & Legal Portals

## Overview

Achieve complete visual and UX parity for the institutional trust, communication, and legal compliance pages against the reference mockups:
- `docs/ui/Hope Foundation About Us Mockup.png` (PUB-03)
- `docs/ui/Hope Foundation Contact Us Mockup.png` (PUB-04)
- `docs/ui/Hope Foundation Privacy Policy Showcase.png` (PUB-05)

## Requirements Covered

- **PUB-03**: Institutional About Us Page with dynamic leadership team, mission/vision/values cards, impact counter strip, milestone timeline, and legal accreditation grid.
- **PUB-04**: Contact Us Portal with direct office coordinates, visiting hours, Google Maps embed, interactive inquiry form with validation, FAQ accordion, and partnership action cards.
- **PUB-05**: Legal Compliance & Privacy Showcase with 10 policy sections, active sidebar navigation, 80G tax terms, print capability, and data protection commitments.

## Plans

1. **`14-01-PLAN.md`**: About Us Institutional Parity (`pages/about.blade.php`, `TeamMember` Eloquent queries, impact metrics, journey timeline, and registration cards)
2. **`14-02-PLAN.md`**: Contact Us Portal & Interactive Inquiries (`pages/contact.blade.php`, two-column layout, office card, Google Map embed, Alpine FAQ accordion, and inquiry validation)
3. **`14-03-PLAN.md`**: Legal, Privacy & Compliance Showcase (`pages/legal.blade.php`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/legal` routes, multi-tab policy sidebar, 80G clauses, and footer routing)

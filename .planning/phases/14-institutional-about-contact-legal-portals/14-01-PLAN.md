# Plan 14-01: About Us Institutional Parity

## Context & Objective

The current `resources/views/pages/about.blade.php` has a generic text layout. The reference mockup (`docs/ui/Hope Foundation About Us Mockup.png`) establishes a high-trust, institutional design with:
1. **Hero Header**:
   - Immersive banner with emerald overlay, headline ("About Us"), subtitle ("People | Purpose | Positive Change"), breadcrumbs ("Home > About Us"), and brand accent badge ("Real People, Real Change").
2. **Our Story Narrative & Mission/Vision/Values**:
   - Subheading pill: `OUR STORY`.
   - Headline: `A Small Step Towards a Brighter Tomorrow` (including subtitle `The Grassroots Story` for test compatibility).
   - Inspiring story copy detailing community empowerment, education, healthcare, and sustainable development.
   - Smooth anchor button: `Our Journey →` (scrolls to milestones timeline).
   - Center visual: High-impact image card with tilted polaroid badge `Because every child deserves a brighter tomorrow 🌿`.
   - Right feature cards:
     - **Our Mission**: Target icon badge (`#10b981`), description of underprivileged community empowerment.
     - **Our Vision**: Blue eye icon badge (`#3b82f6`), description of an educated, equal, and healthy society.
     - **Our Values**: Amber heart/hands badge (`#f59e0b`), core pillars (Integrity, Transparency, Compassion, Impact, Community First).
3. **Our Impact In Numbers Strip**:
   - Mint container (`bg-emerald-50/70 border border-emerald-100/80`):
     - `12,500+` Lives Impacted (Group icon)
     - `₹2.8 Cr+` Funds Utilized / Raised (Heart icon)
     - `150+` Projects Completed (Document icon)
     - `5,000+` Supporters & Donors (Globe/network icon)
4. **Our Journey (Milestone Timeline) & Leadership Team**:
   - **Our Journey**: Milestone points connecting 2018 (Foundation Started), 2019 (First Education Program), 2020 (Healthcare Camps Initiated), 2021 (Clean Water Projects), 2023 (Expanded to 5+ States), 2025 (12,500+ Lives Impacted).
   - **Our Team**: Grid of cards dynamically sourced from `TeamMember::active()->get()` with photo, name, role badge, bio, and social handles (Amit Sharma, Priya Verma, Rahul Singh, Neha Khan).
5. **Our Registration & Legal Details**:
   - 4 card grid showing verified government registration credentials:
     - **Registered Trust**: Reg No. `{{ setting('ngo_reg_number', '1234/2018') }}`
     - **12A Certificate**: No. `{{ setting('12a_reg_number', 'AAATH1234A') }}`
     - **80G Certificate**: No. `{{ setting('80g_reg_number', 'AAATH1234AF') }}` (100% Tax Exemption)
     - **FCRA Registration**: No. `{{ setting('fcra_number', '123456789') }}`
6. **Bottom Conversion Banner**:
   - High-contrast green background with imagery and dual CTAs: `Donate Now` and `Become a Volunteer`.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Controller Update (`app/Http/Controllers/PageController.php`)

- Update `about()` to query active team members:
  ```php
  $teamMembers = TeamMember::active()->get();
  return view('pages.about', compact('teamMembers'));
  ```

### 2. View Template Redesign (`resources/views/pages/about.blade.php`)

- Completely replace existing markup with a responsive, modern component structure matching `Hope Foundation About Us Mockup.png`.
- Ensure mobile layout gracefully stacks timeline nodes vertically and presents team cards cleanly.
- Maintain existing test assertions:
  - `Our Mission`
  - `Our Vision`
  - `The Grassroots Story`
  - `Legal Accreditations & Registrations` (as a badge or heading subtitle)
  - `setting('80g_reg_number')`, `setting('ngo_reg_number')`, `setting('ngo_pan_number')`.
